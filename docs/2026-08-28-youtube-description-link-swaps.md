## Why this exists

Only **3 of your 68 published videos** currently send clicks to
`go.agrolloo.com`. The other 65 put `agrolloo.com/<tool>` (or the raw
affiliate URL) straight in the description, so their clicks are recorded by
Pretty Links in WordPress and never reach the tracker. That is why the
tracker shows ~57 clicks against 170,839 channel views: it is not
undercounting, it is only watching three videos.

Swapping a description to the short link gives you three things the old link
cannot: per-video click attribution in the tracker, one place to change a
destination when an affiliate URL changes, and the daily link guard checking
it. The destination the viewer lands on does not change.

## What to do

For each video below, open it in YouTube Studio and replace the **Old link**
with the **New link**. Nothing else in the description needs to change.

Videos are ordered by view count, so working top-down recovers the most
click data first. The top 10 videos are most of the traffic.

**Every short link below already exists and is live** — no minting needed.
