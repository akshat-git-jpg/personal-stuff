# Subject / identity rules — who to feature

Tell Claude who appears in your footage. It reads this before every edit.

## You (the creator)
- Describe yourself so Claude can recognize you in frames (hair, beard, glasses, usual clothing).
- Which is your good side? Any framing preferences?

## Recurring people
- Name + how to recognize them + when they should/shouldn't be featured.

## General principle (keep this)
- Confirm identity from the actual frame, never the metadata tag.
- Match the subject to the line: "we/us" → people together; "I/me" → you solo.
