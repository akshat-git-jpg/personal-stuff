## Tools covered

Pictory · InVideo · Fliki · Synthesia · Lumen5

## Features

| Tool | Dashboard | Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Pictory | Intuitive, non-technical friendly | Script to video, article to video, edit video using text, visual to video |
| InVideo | Neat, clean | Large ready-made template library, AI text-to-video (prompt → script → video), stock media, editor |
| Fliki | Least intuitive of the five, learnable | Feature set comparable to Pictory/InVideo, plus voice cloning |
| Synthesia | Easy to use | Large AI avatar library, many languages/accents, text-to-speech, custom avatars, gestures, voice cloning, appearance customisation |
| Lumen5 | Friendly | Blog/text to video (no AI voiceover, fewer options), talking head (fewer options than Synthesia, no AI voiceover) |

## Pros and cons

- **Pictory** — pros: automatic filler-word removal, user-friendly, accurate
  stock-footage matching from script, realistic AI voiceovers, team collaboration
  on the top plan. Cons: few ready-made templates for shorts/reels, priced high
  against peers.
- **InVideo** — pros: most affordable, very large short-form template library,
  user-friendly, 24/7 support. Cons: AI voiceover less realistic, stock-footage
  matching less accurate (costs editing time).
- **Fliki** — pros: strong voice cloning, brand customisation (logo, fonts,
  colour scheme). Cons: costly against competitors, credit system hard to budget
  against, stock-footage matching not accurate.
- **Synthesia** — pros: user-friendly, affordable, large avatar range,
  customisable appearance/voice/expressions, wide language coverage, templates.
  Cons: limited expression range, voices can read artificial, limited gestures.
- **Lumen5** — pros: covers both text-to-video and talking head, good template
  range. Cons: no AI voiceover, costly, limited customisation, video length
  capped at 10 minutes on every plan.

## Customer support

- Email only — Pictory, Fliki
- Live chat + email — InVideo, Synthesia
- Helpline + email — Lumen5

## Pricing

Split three ways by use case.

**Long-form faceless** (Pictory, InVideo, Fliki, Lumen5)

- Free tiers: Pictory 3 videos up to 10 min; InVideo 60/month, limited features,
  watermarked, **view-only — no download**; Fliki 5 min/month, watermarked;
  Lumen5 forever-free, 720p, watermarked.
- Pictory: Standard $23 (30 videos, 10 min, 1 user) · Premium $47 (60 videos,
  20 min) · Teams $199 (90 videos, 30 min, 3 users)
- InVideo: 7-day money-back guarantee. Business $15 (60 videos, 40 min, limited
  customisation) · Unlimited $30 (unlimited videos, 40 min)
- Fliki: Standard $28 (180 min credits, 15 min videos) · Business $88 (600 min
  credits, 30 min videos, voice cloning)
- Lumen5: Basic $29 · Starter $79 · Professional $199 · Enterprise custom.
  10-minute cap and no AI voiceover on every tier.
- **Like-for-like:** 60 videos/month costs roughly $47 on Pictory vs $30 on
  InVideo.

**Short-form** (Pictory, InVideo, Fliki) — Pictory and Fliki carry limited
short-form templates at higher prices; InVideo carries a far larger library for
less.

**Talking head** (Synthesia, Lumen5) — Synthesia: 14-day free trial (limited,
watermarked), Personal $24.05 (10 min videos, 90+ avatars, 20 languages, AI
script assistant). Lumen5 as above. Synthesia's cheapest plan carries more than
Lumen5's Professional tier. Yearly billing carries an extra standing discount on
both.

## Final recommendations

| Use case | Pick |
|---|---|
| High-quality long-form faceless | Pictory — voiceover realism + footage matching |
| Long-form faceless on a budget | InVideo — templates, stock, single-prompt generation |
| Voice cloning needed | Fliki |
| Short-form / Reels | InVideo — template volume nothing else matches |
| Talking head / AI presenter | Synthesia — avatar and language range |
