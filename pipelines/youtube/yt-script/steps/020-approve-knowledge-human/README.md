# 020 - approve the knowledge

**[OWNER]** &nbsp; You pick the approach, fill the gaps it found, then say go.

The owner reads 010's report, picks which approach the video teaches, answers the gap questions, and confirms the knowledge is complete enough to build on. Nothing is written until he does. A gap left unanswered here becomes an invented claim later, and an approach left unchosen here cannot be chosen later without rewriting the outline.

**Reads:** `knowledge.md`

**Gate:** the knowledge is complete enough to build a video on, and the approach is chosen

---

## What 010 must put in front of you

Four parts, in this order, in one message. This is a contract - 010 does not get
to reorder it or drop a part.

1. **The 5-line summary** - what the video is about. Five lines, hard cap.
2. **The format call** - tutorial or comparison, stated, with a one-line reason.
   **Not a question.** 010 decides; you override here or at gate 040.
3. **The approaches menu** - where the topic has candidate approaches: the
   table, the splice-in techniques, and 010's recommendation. Absent only if
   010 says plainly that it skipped the section, and why.
4. **The gaps** - as questions.

If a part is missing, ask for it before answering anything. A short message is
not the goal here; a decidable one is.

## What you do

1. **Pick the approach** - one row, a mix of rows, or something new built from
   the splice-in techniques. This locks step 030's section list, because a
   tutorial's sections are the phases of the approach you picked.
2. **Answer the gaps** - supply what is missing, or say a gap is fine to leave.
3. **Say go.**

You can also reject the format call here. Doing it now is free.

## Why this is a gate

Every claim downstream traces to `knowledge.md` and nothing else. A gap you skip
here does not stay a gap - it becomes a sentence somebody wrote from general
knowledge, in a video with your name on it.

And the approach is not a detail you can revisit at 055. By then the outline is
approved, the section names are locked, and the beat document is written against
them. Changing the approach after that is not an edit, it is a rewrite.
