# How to Make Vox Style Videos with AI

Format: tutorial

## Intro
- hook: a finished Vox-style shot plays first, then the reveal that no frame of it touched After Effects and no board was designed by hand
- credibility: one topic taken end to end through OpenArt Director, in one chat, with every setting and every failure shown on screen
- roadmap: names the eight sections below

## Body
1. What Makes It Vox — the five things every Vox shot has, so you know what you are recreating before opening anything
2. Why One Chat — what OpenArt Director does that a stack of five tools does not, and the one alternative worth knowing about
3. The Script — the builder template, the three things it must be fed, and editing the draft inline before locking it
4. Locking the Style — generating boards, rejecting the generic first pass, and approving the set that every later scene inherits
5. Casting Voice and Music — auditioning ten narrators and ten tracks, and laying them over the boards to get the timing base
6. Adding Motion — simple motion per board timed to the voice, then the stitch, and why more animation makes it worse
7. When It Breaks — the three failures you will hit, and the fix for each without losing your good takes
8. Cost and Limits — what this actually costs, where the output still shows its seams, and the YouTube risk nobody mentions

## Conclusion
- what to do next on your own topic, and the one thing to get right first
