The main issue revolves around credits. What even is a credit, and how does it work? Well, credits are basically the way that multimodal sites like Freepik charge for AI generations. Whenever you generate something with an AI model, the site that you're using, whether that's Freepik, Higgsfield, or Artlist, has to request that generation from whoever designed the AI model. That costs money. If you're using Nano Banana for an image through Higgsfield, Higgsfield has to pay Google for your generation, and then they get that generation, and they give it back to you. The amount of credits that you get each month with your Higgsfield subscription dictates how many AI generations of a certain quality you'll be able to create in that time. You could get a couple thousand super cheap low-quality images, or you could get a handful of really high-quality videos. Credits just standardize the whole thing. The problem is credits aren't standardized across every site. So, a Nano Banana Pro generation from Higgsfield can cost two credits, and the same generation costs 400 credits on Artlist. Multiply that by all six major sites, and the result is a massive headache, especially when you throw in the fact that all of these options have multiple subscription prices, and many of them also have unlimited generation features that are even more confusing.
