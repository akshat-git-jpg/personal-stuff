Why v1 failed

Every version so far, including yesterday’s sketches, was a metaphor — a gate, a chain of boxes, a queue, a maze, an engine in cables, a peeled poster. A metaphor has to be worked out, and the viewer gets three seconds. Making a metaphor smaller doesn’t help; it is still a metaphor. That is why “simplify it” kept not landing.

The rule from now on

A visual is allowed only if it needs zero interpretation. Two things qualify:

  • The words being spoken, appearing as they are spoken
  • The real thing named — an actual tool logo, an actual screen

Nothing else goes on screen without asking you first.

What this costs

About an hour. No image generation at all — it is type and timing, so there is nothing to render externally and nothing to approve twice.

What gets deleted

The gate, the chain of boxes, the tool tab panel, the queue, the measuring rule. Every invented object leaves the film.

If a line is wrong

Say the beat and the words you want instead. The words are the whole design now, so changing them is a one-line edit.
